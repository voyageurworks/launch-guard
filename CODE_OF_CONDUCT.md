# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to keep their private data private. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to git, security or open source;
- argue about the code and the behaviour, not the person;
- accept that maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions and comments that break these rules;
- never post anyone's private information, including your own secrets, in the project's spaces.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not welcome and lead to removal from the project.

To report a conduct problem, use the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct". That keeps it private. Reports are handled by the maintainers, and the person reporting is not named to anyone else.
